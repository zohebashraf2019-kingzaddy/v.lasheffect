Meet Valery

The artist behind V. Lash Effect.

Hi I’m Valery, the owner behind V.Lash Effect 🪄

I’ve been in the beauty industry for over 3 years and I absolutely love what I do.

When I was choosing a name for my business, I wanted it to represent more than just lashes. The “Effect” isn’t just the set you leave with. It’s the confidence, the simplicity of feeling put together, and enhancing the beauty that’s already there.

My goal is for every client to leave my studio feeling like the best version of themselves with a set that’s customized just for them!

Thank you for trusting me with your lashes and I can’t wait to meet all the new faces to come 💌
